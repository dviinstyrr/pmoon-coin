🌕 Power Moon (PMOON)

Power Moon (PMOON) is a community-powered crypto token designed to bring real-world impact through blockchain. Built on principles of transparency, halal ethics, and decentralized collaboration, PMOON introduces a sustainable and ethical approach to crypto — one that empowers both investors and communities.

🔁 Circle Impact Model

PMOON operates on a unique circle impact structure, where part of the project’s liquidity and capital gains is reinvested into:

Strategic Investments

Supporting growth-stage companies and stocks that contribute to innovation and employment.

Infrastructure Development

Rebuilding and improving essential structures in selected underserved regions.

Charity & Education

Funding scholarships, medical aid, and humanitarian causes.

🛡️ Key Features

Halal-Compliant Framework

No gambling, no riba. Clear structure for developer earnings and ethical reinvestment.

Transparent Tokenomics

Locked liquidity, public smart contracts, and open community governance.

Multi-Utility Ecosystem

Use cases include staking, NFT integration, governance, and ecosystem rewards.

Emotional Branding

Featuring a friendly mascot named Moo, creating familiarity and emotional connection.

🌍 Vision

To become a beacon of positive change through crypto, proving that blockchain can empower, uplift, and unite people — not just generate profits.

🎯 Mission

To build a decentralized, ethical, and impactful crypto ecosystem driven by community trust and innovation.
